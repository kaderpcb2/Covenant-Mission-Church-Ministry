Why We Are Here

We exist to anchor lives in the unchanging love of God. Covenant Christian Ministry is here to be a spiritual home where you can heal, discover your God-given purpose, and build lasting friendships. Ultimately, we are here to honor God by loving people, sharing hope, and making a tangible difference in our community and the world.

Our Mission

To share the unconditional love of Jesus Christ, build authentic Christian community, and equip believers to serve our neighborhood.

Our Core Beliefs

To connect people with God's love, grow together in Christ, and transform our world through service.
